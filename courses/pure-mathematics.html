Calculus: Differentiation

Learn how to find derivatives of functions using first principles and rules.

Power Rule
If \( f(x) = x^n \), then \( f'(x) = nx^{n-1} \)

Example:

Differentiate \( f(x) = 3x^4 + 2x^2 - 5 \)

Using the power rule:

$$ f'(x) = 3 \cdot 4x^{3} + 2 \cdot 2x^{1} - 0 $$ $$ f'(x) = 12x^3 + 4x $$
